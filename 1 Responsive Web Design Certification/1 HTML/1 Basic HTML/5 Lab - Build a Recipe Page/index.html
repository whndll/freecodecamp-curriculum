Classic Pancake Batter

A simple and fluffy homemade pancake batter made from fresh eggs, milk, and flour, whisked together for the perfect morning treat.

Ingredients

  • 3 large fresh eggs
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. Place the flour into a large mixing bowl.
  2. Add the three eggs and pour in the milk gradually.
  3. Whisk the ingredients together vigorously until the batter is smooth and lump-free.
  4. Pour the batter onto a hot, buttered skillet and cook until golden brown on both sides.
